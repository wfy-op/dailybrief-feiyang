中文摘要摘要 我们展示了两种无需外延再生长制造的 GaN 基面发射激光器（SEL）的室温脉冲激射。我们呈现了圆形光栅（CGSEL）和光子晶体（PCSEL）设计之间的直接比较。这些器件通过将光子结构直接刻蚀到 p-GaN 包层中，并利用图案化氧化铟锡（ITO）顶部接触来实现。两种设计在脉冲电流注入下均在约 438 nm 附近表现出激射。包含中心缺陷的 CGSEL 通过耦合到带隙缺陷模，实现了低阈值电流密度（<1 kA/cm 2）和小发散角（≈0.15°）。相比之下，PCSEL 显示出更高的阈值电流密度，并在 1D 带边模上激射，从而产生十字形远场图案。这些结果证实了无再生长方法是可制造 GaN SELs 的一种可行路径。关键的是，该比较研究确定 CGSEL 缺陷模设计是在低限制外延结构中实现高性能激射的更稳健路径。

英文摘要Abstract We demonstrate room-temperature pulsed lasing of two types of GaN-based surface emitting lasers (SEL) fabricated without epitaxial regrowth. We present a direct comparison between a circular grating (CGSEL) and a photonic crystal (PCSEL) design. The devices are realized by etching the photonic structures directly into the p-GaN cladding, and utilizing a patterned indium tin oxide (ITO) top contact. Both designs exhibit lasing near 438 nm under pulsed current injection. The CGSEL, incorporating a central defect, achieves a low threshold current density (&lt;1 kA/cm 2 ) and a small divergence angle (≈0.15°) by coupling to a bandgap defect mode. In contrast, the PCSEL shows a higher threshold current density and lases on a 1D band-edge mode, resulting in a cross-shaped far-field pattern. These results confirm the regrowth-free method as a viable route for manufacturable GaN SELs. Crucially, the comparative study identifies the CGSEL defect-mode design as a more robust path toward high-performance lasing in low-confinement epitaxial structures.
