中文摘要光子晶体（PC）是实现表面发射量子级联激光器的优秀方案。光子晶体阵列的尺寸与成本和散热都有密切关系。我们报道了波长为 9.4 μm、具有紧凑 PC 尺寸的量子级联光子晶体表面发射激光器（QC-PCSELs），展示了 140 mW 的峰值输出功率。PC 结构通过从表面进行深刻蚀工艺制备。通过引入椭圆结构 PC 并调节轴比和填充因子，面内对称性被打破，Q factors 得到调控，并优化了输出功率和单模性能。并且，通过引入横向耦合腔来补偿寄生损耗，可以降低阈值电流。此外，我们通过一种特殊的聚焦离子束（FIB）二次刻蚀研究了 PC 深度对激光器性能的影响。结果表明，可以精确优化深度以获得最大输出功率以及相应降低的阈值电流密度和提高的斜率效率。

英文摘要Photonic crystal (PC) is an excellent solution to realize a surface-emitting quantum cascade laser. The size of photonic crystal array has a severe relevance with both cost and heat dissipation. We report quantum cascade photonic crystal surface-emitting lasers (QC-PCSELs) at 9.4 μm with compact PC dimension, demonstrating a peak output power of 140 mW. The PC structure is fabricated by deep-etching process from the surface. Through introducing an elliptical structure PC and adjusting the axis ratio and filling factor, in-plane symmetry is broken and the Q factors have been tuned, and the output power and single-mode performance have been optimized. And by introducing lateral coupling cavity to compensate the parasitic loss, the threshold current can be reduced. Furthermore, we investigate the influence of PC depth on the performance of laser by a special focused ion beam (FIB) secondary etching. The results show the depth can be precisely optimized to obtain the maximum output power and the corresponding reduced threshold current density and increased slope efficiency.
